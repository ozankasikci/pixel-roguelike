# Quick Task 260329-t50: Fix Lighting Attenuation and Intensity Summary

**One-liner:** Physically-based 1/r^2 inverse-square attenuation added to scene.frag with window cutoff, all scene light intensities rescaled ~7x to compensate, exposure raised to 1.4, and Metal specular denominator floor raised to prevent grazing-angle spikes.

## What Was Done

### Task 1: Shader Fix

In `assets/shaders/game/scene.frag`:

- **attenuation() function** (line 72-77): Added `invSq = 1.0 / (dist * dist + 0.01)` multiplied by the existing window term. The 0.01 epsilon prevents division-by-zero at dist=0 and caps peak attenuation at 100. The window term (`clamp(1 - pow(dist/radius, 4))^2`) still provides smooth cutoff at the light radius boundary.

- **Specular denominator** (line 726): Raised floor from `0.001` to `0.01`, capping specular at 100x instead of 1000x to prevent extreme white spikes on Metal materials at grazing angles.

### Task 2: Intensity Rescaling

With `invSq` at medium range (dist=3-5m), effective attenuation drops from ~0.99 to ~0.04-0.11 — approximately a 10-25x reduction. A ~7x compensation factor was applied across all light sources:

**RuntimeSceneRenderer.cpp:**
- `kPlayerTorchIntensity`: 0.07 -> 0.5
- `kPlayerTorchSpillIntensity`: 1.12 -> 8.0
- `kPlayerTorchHaloIntensity`: 0.54 -> 4.0
- `kPlayerHandGlowIntensity`: 0.04 -> 0.3

**PostProcessParams.h:**
- `exposure`: 1.02 -> 1.4

**assets/scenes/warden_office.scene:**
- spot_light intensity: 2.0 -> 14.0
- light intensities: 0.6->4.2, 0.5->3.5, 0.3->2.1

**assets/scenes/silos_cloister.scene:**
- light intensities: 0.22->1.54, 0.26->1.82, 0.22->1.54
- dir_light left unchanged (directional lights bypass point attenuation)

**assets/scenes/cathedral.scene:**
- All 13 point/spot lights multiplied by 7x

**assets/prefabs/gameplay/checkpoint.prefab:**
- light intensity: 1.15 -> 8.05
